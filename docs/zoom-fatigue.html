Zoom Fatigue — Classroom Edition (8s loop)

For students & teachers: fresh → drained → recover. Play in class, then try the fix together.

TL;DR — Staring + seeing yourself + no movement = tired eyes & foggy brain. Fix: 20-20-20, stretch, water, camera breaks.

👁 Eyes

Blink less on screen. Every 20 min, look 20 ft away for 20 sec.

🧍 Body

Stand, roll shoulders, unclench jaw. Still images hide fidget needs.

📷 Mind

Hide self-view, allow cameras-off breaks. Quiet class ≠ lazy class.
